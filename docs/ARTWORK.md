# Artwork and template credits

OpenCanvas is an independent design editor by Mathew Minguel, built with assistance from OpenAI Codex. Its template layouts are original compositions. The gallery keeps the design previews uncluttered; this page records artwork origins and links to licenses.

## Photo and illustrated collections

The new [Open Library](LIBRARY-EXPANSION.md) includes 106 credited outside photos and artworks with per-item CC0, public-domain or CC BY records in [its manifest](../assets/open-library/manifest.json). [Open sources](OPEN-SOURCES.md) records research and license evidence. CC BY template exports include visible attribution linked to the bundled credits page. [Signal Lab](LIQUID-SIGNAL.md) adds four generated chrome/glass assets with exact prompts in [its provenance file](../assets/signal/PROVENANCE.json).

Images in `assets/people/` were generated with OpenAI's image generation tool for this project. People, events and places depicted in these generated scenes are illustrative, not documentary photographs or endorsements. The complete [image manifest](../assets/people/PROVENANCE.json) records each image, its prompt, creation method and processing notes. Templates retain image-origin descriptions in their image layers.

Some designs use only editable text and geometric shapes. A template's photo can be replaced with your own image in the editor. A photo shown in multiple layouts remains the same source image; layout counts are not counts of unique photos.

## Community templates and graphics

- [Community source manifest](../community-sources/PROVENANCE.json): creator names, source links and reuse evidence for the included community templates. Their **Creator & license** buttons remain available on cards.
- [Lucide graphics](../assets/lucide/PROVENANCE.json): six open-source icons, with the complete [upstream license](../assets/lucide/LICENSE). Native SVGs and portable PNG renderings are included.
- [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and [project rights](../RIGHTS.md) describe other included materials and project-wide terms.

## Design collections and visual research

- [Signature](SIGNATURE.md): 20 original compositions built from editable type and native shapes, with no new AI imagery or stock photos.
- [Art Directed](ART-DIRECTED.md): 24 individually composed designs, six new transparent/texture/photo assets, and five open font families. Exact [prompts and provenance](../assets/atelier/PROVENANCE.json) are included.
- [Canva comparison](CANVA-COMPARISON.md): public gallery research, existing features, and remaining product gaps.
- [Nightlife & Culture](NEW-WAVE.md): 48 layouts using eight new generated images, with research and prompt links.
- [Flyer Lab](FLYER-LAB.md): 36 original music, nightlife and community flyers, using a broader mix of existing artwork and editable graphic designs.

[Canva music flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/music/) and [Canva party flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/party/) were reviewed as visual references for genre variety, contrasting type, event hierarchy and photo-led promotions. No Canva source code, template files, paid assets or watermarked previews were copied into OpenCanvas.


## Gaming Studio and Everyday Stories

[Gaming Studio](GAMING.md) includes 20 native editable compositions and four original generated illustrations, with separate editable typography. The [Gaming asset manifest](../assets/gaming/PROVENANCE.json) records exact prompts and saved paths. Higgsfield submitted no generation jobs because the connected account plan did not support them; the four illustrations were made using OpenAI’s built-in image generator. They are illustrative artwork, not real people or official game/team assets.

[Everyday Stories](CATEGORY-COLLECTION.md) adds 26 original compositions built entirely from editable native text and shapes. No new raster or AI artwork is used in that collection. The [category audit](CATEGORY-AUDIT.md) records the category research, counts and limits.
