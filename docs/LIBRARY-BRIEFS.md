# Creative Library: 650 original briefs

This expansion supplies **26 useful briefs in every one of OpenCanvas's 25 topic categories**. The composition engine gives each brief four distinct layout treatments, for 104 new entries per category and 2,600 entries across the topic library. A brief is a subject and its editable copy; it is not itself a distinct photograph or layout. The separate Liquid Signal collection is not included in those figures.

The purpose of the expansion is breadth with usable starting points. A pet sitter needs different information from an adoption event. A printmaker needs a different workshop description from a weaver. Those distinctions are written into the content rather than produced by changing a generic event title.

## Data contract

`library-briefs.js` exposes `libraryTopics`, an array in the same topic order as the navigation. Each item contains `topic`, a stable URL-friendly `slug`, and 26 `briefs`. Each brief supplies:

- `title`: a short headline, no more than 24 characters.
- `subtitle`: a specific use case or service description.
- `kicker`: its topic label.
- `details`: three short, individually editable information lines.
- `cta`: a meaningful action, such as booking a session, sharing a story or visiting a market.
- `visual`: a subject keyword for appropriate artwork selection.
- `tags`: category, imagery and use-case phrases for search.

Names, dates, venues, prices and contact information are clearly editable placeholders where needed. The collection does not invent past event dates, working contact addresses, statistics, prices or product claims. The native source uses compact authored rows to keep all 650 briefs easy to review; these are expanded into plain objects at load time.

## Research and editorial direction

Research was refreshed on **14 October 2026** using Canva's public category galleries. These references establish breadth of use cases, not permission to redistribute the files displayed there. No Canva source code, template file, copy block or branded artwork was imported into this brief collection. All titles, descriptions and calls to action here were independently written.

The four priorities in the user's screenshots received the most detailed review:

- [Canva's pet gallery](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/pet/) includes adoption, care, grooming, sitting, shop materials and missing-animal notices. The new pet set expands those purposes across several companion-animal types and includes practical details specific to each service.
- [Craft templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/craft/) and the [additional craft gallery](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/craft/?continuation=100) show workshops, maker events and a range of craft disciplines. Our new set names the actual medium and activity, including weaving, visible mending, bookbinding and linocut.
- [Photography flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/photography/) cover services, pricing, exhibitions, portfolios and learning sessions. Our 26 briefs deliberately span subject genres and practical business uses so photography is not only a gallery-opening category.
- [Technology templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/technology/) and the [tech gallery](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/tech/) include product communication, events, presentations and diagrams. Our new set adds specific communities and tasks: defensive security, data visualization, accessibility, repair, cloud and prototype feedback.

Other public galleries were checked to broaden the topic library:

- [Music flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/music/) cover both small performances and larger event promotion. Our music content adds listening cultures, specific genres and participation formats.
- [Podcast covers](https://www.canva.com/podcasts/templates/) combine host portraits, typography, subject and show branding. Our briefs extend the promotional lifecycle to guests, trailers, live recordings and follow-up discussions.
- [Fitness flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/fitness/) cover classes, coaching, schedules and varied activities. [Wellness event templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/wellness-event/?continuation=300) and [retreat templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/retreat/?continuation=350) extend the range to quieter activities and planning.
- [Beauty templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/beauty/) and [food flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/food/) provide the category reference points for service and product promotions. New copy was developed around actual booking and menu information.
- [Education flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/education/) include classroom notices, school events and student opportunities. [Book templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/book/) add book clubs, fairs and reading-related communication.
- [Real-estate flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/real-estate/) include property listings, open houses and contact-led information. [Wedding invitations](https://www.canva.com/wedding-invitations/templates/) establish invitation use cases; our expansion also supplies matching information-card subjects.
- [Volunteer flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/volunteer/) and [fundraiser flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/fundraiser/) distinguish recruitment, collections, community events and cause promotion.
- [Sports flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/sports/) cover several sports and event types. [Garden templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/garden/) combine practical growing communication and garden events.
- [Business flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/business/), [marketing flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/marketing/) and [advertising flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/advertising/) show the value of a specific audience, clear offer and useful next action.
- [Party flyers](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/party/) span seasonal and milestone celebrations. Canva's [entertainment collection](https://www.canva.com/entertainment/) also includes production materials; this release adds flyer subjects for screenings, crew gatherings and performance opportunities, not full production-document functionality.
- [Travel templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/travel/) and [clothing templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/clothes/) were used as further category references. Our travel and fashion lists are independently expanded around routes, services, materials and creative practices.
- Gaming use cases also build on the [prior category audit](CATEGORY-AUDIT.md), which inspected Canva's [Gaming posters](https://www.canva.com/posters/templates/gaming/) and [Gaming banners](https://www.canva.com/banners/templates/gaming/?continuation=100).

Search indexes occasionally include unrelated items inside a category. We used the relevant purposes rather than blindly importing a result list or treating the displayed count as an exact count of distinct designs.

## Verification and limits

`tests/library-briefs.test.cjs` verifies the complete topic coverage, 650-brief total, unique titles and purposes within each category, renderer contract, concise copy, searchable disciplines, and absence of fake contact information or dated events. It also checks that the four priority categories contain the requested subject breadth.

The four treatments per brief are alternate compositions of one useful subject. These counts do not mean 2,600 independent photo shoots, source artists or entirely unrelated design languages. Artwork attribution and generation records belong to the relevant asset manifests; this file describes the authored copy and topic taxonomy only. Rendering, export, selection and gallery checks are handled by the collection integration tests.
