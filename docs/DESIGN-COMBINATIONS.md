# Design Combinations — source notes

Reviewed on 12 October 2026. This addition contains **120 original theme/style combinations**, each adapted to five formats: portrait social post, square post, story/reel cover, flyer and landscape cover. That is **600 new template entries**, bringing the complete gallery to **913**. The count does not represent 600 unrelated designs or new photographs.

## Reference research

- [Canva templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/): the public gallery lists photo collages, minimalist fashion posts, retro opening flyers, studio and exhibition flyers, newspaper/typographic posters and geometric story layouts. These broad categories informed the mix of promotional purposes and composition styles.
- [Modern Rave Posters on Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com/mbg555/modern-rave-posters/): the public board describes abstract/acid graphics, high-contrast concert posters, typography-heavy designs, tape imagery and geometric compositions. It served as a discovery reference. Pinterest's homepage was unavailable to the research fetch; the public board was accessible.

No Canva source code, template files, screenshots, branded content, or Pinterest image assets were downloaded into this pack. These references are inspiration, not a license or a claim of partnership. New symbols are original geometric constructions authored directly in `remix-core.js`.

## What was authored

- Five art directions: Acid grid, Cut-paper, Swiss type, Retro stamp and Gallery frame.
- 24 themes spanning underground rave, UK garage, amapiano, jungle, disco, ambient, rooftop, hard techno, synthwave, concerts, comedy, cinema, vintage markets, beauty, streetwear, podcasts, creators, retail sales, photography, workshops, fitness, wellness, cafes and community events.
- 24 symbol sets: sparks, rays, orbit, record badge, checkerboard, halftone, sound levels, arrow, flower, target, pixel steps, cross, asterisk, diamonds, cloud, waves, corners, ticket, cassette, speaker, moon, sprig, windows and confetti.
- Symbols insert as multiple native shape layers, which remain independently editable. One Undo removes one complete symbol insertion. Background-colored masking pieces are editable shapes too.
- Photos reuse the existing generated assets documented in [image provenance](../assets/people/PROVENANCE.json). Only photo-based combinations carry the AI imagery badge; geometric designs carry an Editable shapes badge.

The gallery interleaves topics and directions before repeating a theme. Format, topic, style, imagery and layout filters can be combined. Project-wide ownership and reuse terms remain in [RIGHTS.md](../RIGHTS.md).
