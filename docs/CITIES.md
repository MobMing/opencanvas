# City Collection

24 editable designs across New York, Chicago, Miami, London, Paris, Tokyo, Barcelona and Winston-Salem. Each destination includes a portrait poster, square social-event flyer and landscape travel cover. Text, colors, shapes and image layers remain editable.

Search for `cities`, `city`, a destination, `NYC`, or `Winston Salem`. City results appear first on Home; choose **New: Cities** in the template gallery to see only these 24 designs.
