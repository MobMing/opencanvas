# Flyer Lab

Added 14 October 2026: 36 original editable templates across 12 composition families and four formats. There are 27 photo-led designs using 34 distinct existing credited artwork assets, plus nine designs made entirely from editable text and geometric shapes. These are new layouts and combinations, not 34 newly generated photographs.

## Composition families

The Art Directed release rebuilds all 36 compositions with full-bleed photo framing, larger condensed headlines, editorial typography, layered graphic details, and native photo crops. Existing template IDs, names, and imagery are preserved so bookmarks and search results continue to work. See [Art Directed](ART-DIRECTED.md) for the 24 additional designs and the new preview and photo-editing tools.

Back-to-back bills, record-shop sleeves, three-frame photo dispatches, after-hours timetables, wide community bulletins, detachable guest passes, vertical artist diaries, orbit sound graphics, acoustic editorials, asymmetric culture zines, festival wayfinding posters and weekend diptychs.

Music and nightlife topics include underground rave, house, techno, disco, vinyl, jazz, live soul, indie concerts and open-air festivals. Other topics include makers markets, dance classes, local creative meetups, exhibitions, fashion and social promotions. Event details and all text remain editable; photo layers can be replaced.

## References and artwork

The public [Canva music flyer](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/music/) and [party flyer](https://www.canva.com/flyers/templates/party/) galleries informed broad ideas such as genre-specific poster styles, contrasting typography, colorful event graphics and photo-led promotions. The compositions, titles and geometric graphics here are original; no Canva template files or paid artwork were imported.

Photos and illustrations come from the project's existing [generated artwork manifest](../assets/people/PROVENANCE.json). They depict fictional people and illustrative scenes. The nine graphic-only designs use native editor shapes and text.

## Gallery presentation

Repeated imagery badges have been removed from template cards. [Artwork and license credits](ARTWORK.md) remain linked from the gallery, and image-origin descriptions remain in editable projects. The gallery mixes all collections while spreading repeated photos and layouts apart; searches and filters keep their scope, and loading more cards preserves the current order.
