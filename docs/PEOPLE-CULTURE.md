# People & Culture

This collection addresses repetitive browsing, not just a larger count. It adds 20 original compositions, each using a different main photograph: eight newly generated scenes and twelve distinct existing assets. Eighteen designs depict people; two feature a crowd or music equipment. The library now contains 933 entries, or 165 concept cards with variations grouped.

The eight new scenes are a DJ duo, full-body dancer, live soul singer, podcast hosts, editorial beauty portrait, nightlife friends, stand-up comedian and creative founder. The full prompts and asset paths are in [PROVENANCE.json](../assets/people/PROVENANCE.json). Created with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, JPEG encoded with no crop. These are fictional adults, not photographs of actual performers or endorsements.

## Design and browsing

Each design has its own native format and independently specified typography, image scale, framing and palette. There are portrait posts, square posts and one story cover; this collection is not multiplied into five size variants. Images retain square proportions and can be replaced. Titles, captions, frames and accents are native editable layers.

The gallery, home recommendations and editor sidebar lead with this collection. Default gallery browsing groups older Campaign Collection sizes and Design Combinations sizes/styles by concept. Filtering runs before grouping; choose a format, layout or style to see matching versions. Show all variations reveals every matching entry. Existing IDs and saved projects remain usable.

No Canva code, template file, photograph or Pinterest artwork was copied. Broad design ideas such as editorial covers, live-performance posters, listening-club sleeves and nightlife snapshots were implemented as original compositions.
