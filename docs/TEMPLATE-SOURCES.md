# Growing the OpenCanvas template library

Research checked 12 October 2026. These are source findings, not partnership agreements with OpenCanvas.

## Where Canva gets its content

- **Template designers:** [Canva Creators](https://www.canva.com/creators/) accepts designers through a portfolio application. Its [template marketplace program](https://www.canva.com/creators/templates/) pays royalties tied to usage. This is a supply of finished editable layouts, distinct from stock photographs.
- **Owned stock libraries:** Canva [acquired Pexels and Pixabay in 2019](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-acquires-pexels-pixabay/). The announcement described over one million additional images and vectors at that time; it is not a current template count. The old announcement's licensing language must not substitute for today's source license.
- **Licensed stock partners:** Canva describes a [Getty Images relationship dating to 2018](https://www.canva.com/en_in/newsroom/news/getty-ai-partnership/), adding images and video and later expanding into AI licensing. Those are Canva's contracts; OpenCanvas receives no rights through them.
- **Branded collections:** Canva's [current content agreement](https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/) includes branded and Disney collections with use restrictions. A Canva subscription does not grant blanket permission to move its library into another editor.

No verified per-partner count of finished templates was established. Stock-media counts and creator counts should not be advertised as template counts.

## Practical sources for OpenCanvas

1. Original editable layouts and generated imagery. The new Campaign Collection contains **48 original campaign concepts in five formats (240 templates)**, using 14 clearly labeled AI-generated assets: varied fictional DJs, a singer, creators, crowds, vinyl equipment, product still life and abstract chrome art. The 12 layout families include scene posters, artist lineups, tickets, zine collages, record sleeves, magazines, contact sheets and typography-led promos. These are format variants of 48 campaigns, not 240 independent photo shoots. Text and shapes are editable; photographs are replaceable raster layers. Prompt records are in `assets/people/PROVENANCE.json`.
2. Direct contributions from designers with permission to redistribute editable templates, including all embedded imagery and fonts. Creator credit and asset provenance should travel with each contribution. No external creator has been contacted or signed up by this change.
3. Individually verified public-domain or CC0 resources and compatible open-license artwork. The existing collection preserves these sources in `community-sources/PROVENANCE.json`. Public access on GitHub, Reddit, or Pinterest alone does not establish a reusable license.
4. A separately agreed stock integration. [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) permits creative uses including templates, but prohibits stock-platform redistribution and implied endorsement. [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license) excludes compilation into a competing service. Review the proposed integration and API conditions before packaging either library; do not scrape their catalogs into this repository.

## New collection contents

Nightlife and DJ promotions, podcast guests, speakers, workshops, creators, agencies, networking, photography, fitness, Pilates, yoga, dance, running clubs, wellness events, studio openings, beauty, salons, fashion, collaborations, pop-up shops, sales, art meetups and live streams.

Formats: 1080×1350 portrait post, 1080×1080 square post, 1080×1920 story/static reel cover, 1275×1650 flyer, and 1280×720 landscape cover. These are static designs, not animated videos.

AI imagery is labeled in discovery, Home previews and photo alternative text. A user can select its image layer and choose **Replace photo**. Photos are embedded in projects for offline editing and export; no remote stock service or API key is needed.
