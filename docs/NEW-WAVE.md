# Nightlife & Culture collection

Added 14 October 2026: 48 editable designs across 12 composition families and four formats, using eight new AI-generated photo/art assets. All event names, dates and venue details are editable placeholders. People and events in the generated images are fictional.

## Visual research

These sources informed broad design directions, not copied template files:

- [Canva templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/), [DJ posters](https://www.canva.com/posters/templates/dj/) and [festival posters](https://www.canva.com/posters/templates/festival/): strong headline hierarchy, artist photography, varied event formats.
- [Reddit event poster critique](https://www.reddit.com/r/graphic_design/comments/1iqo5c9/i_made_this_event_poster_is_it_any_good/) and [show poster discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/graphic_design/comments/1ry9erh/poster_design_i_made_for_a_show/): readable event details, clear venue information and separation between artwork and text.
- [Pinterest modern rave poster board](https://www.pinterest.com/mbg555/modern-rave-posters/): indexed visual references for rave collage and colorful poster directions. No board artwork was downloaded or bundled.
- X/Twitter was searched, but no reusable artwork with verified permission was identified or imported.

## Imported open-source graphics

Six icons from [Lucide on GitHub](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide) are included: music-2, radio, mic-vocal, sparkles, ticket and headphones. Original SVGs, source URLs, checksums and the complete upstream license are in [assets/lucide](../assets/lucide/). The ISC license and included Feather MIT notices are preserved in [LICENSE](../assets/lucide/LICENSE). Small PNG renderings recolored to each design's ink color are bundled for portable project import/export.

## Generated assets

The eight images depict a laser-lit rave, jazz trio, garage band, disco friends, makers market, dancer, chrome sculpture and desert stage. They were created individually with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, in generation mode without input images. Full prompts are in [WAVE-PROMPTS.json](../assets/people/WAVE-PROMPTS.json), with paths and provenance in [PROVENANCE.json](../assets/people/PROVENANCE.json). JPEG encoding preserves each full square composition.

## Discovery

The new collection is mixed into Home, Templates and the editor sidebar. The stable ordering spaces out repeated imagery, layouts and topics while keeping search/filter results relevant. Select **New: Nightlife & Culture · 48** to browse the collection alone. Loading more cards preserves the existing order.
