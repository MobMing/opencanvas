Ten original assets were generated using OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. Complete prompts and file paths are in [the provenance manifest](../assets/people/PROVENANCE.json), under IDs starting `studio-`. Each asset is saved at `assets/people/<id>.jpg` and embedded in the app. Generated people are fictional; the city scene is illustrative, not a verified location photograph. Titles, captions, frames, tape overlays, cassette parts, vinyl rings, graphic patterns and glitch bars are editable layers. Generated imagery is a replaceable flat image layer. Presentation templates are covers, not full slide decks. Glitch and music templates are static artwork, not animated videos. No Canva template files, source code or stock artwork were copied.
